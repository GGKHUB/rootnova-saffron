LATE SUMMER · AUGUST01
01 / PREPARE

Set the ground

Choose a sunny, well-drained bed. Loosen the soil and work in compost so corms can settle into a light, airy home.

SOIL & SUN01 — 06
LATE SUMMER · AUGUST02
02 / PLANT

Nestle in corms

Plant healthy saffron corms point-up, about 10–15 cm deep and spaced apart. A little room helps each one multiply underground.

10–15 CM DEEP02 — 06
AUTUMN · OCTOBER03
03 / AWAKEN

Welcome the bloom

With autumn's cooler air, slender leaves and violet flowers emerge. Keep the bed weed-free and let seasonal rain do its work.

COOLER DAYS03 — 06
AUTUMN · OCTOBER04
04 / GATHER

Pick at first light

Harvest flowers by hand in the cool morning, while petals are still closed. Gentle handling keeps the precious stigmas intact.

HAND-HARVESTED04 — 06
HARVEST DAY · OCTOBER05
05 / SEPARATE

Keep the crimson

Carefully separate the three red stigmas from each flower. This delicate work is done by hand, one bloom at a time.

THREE STIGMAS05 — 06
AFTER HARVEST · OCTOBER06
06 / PRESERVE

Dry. Rest. Savour.

Dry threads gently until fragrant and crisp, then store airtight away from light. A patient rest deepens their colour and aroma.
